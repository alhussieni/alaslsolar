FAQ

Common questions about solar irrigation

Can solar run irrigation pumps at night?

Solar alone cannot — but adding battery storage or keeping a generator on standby allows continuous operation. We design the right combination based on your irrigation schedule.

What happens during cloudy or winter days?

PV systems still generate power on cloudy days at reduced output. A hybrid or battery system compensates for the shortfall without interrupting operations.

Do I need to stop using my generator?

No. A hybrid system keeps the generator as backup while solar handles the base load — reducing runtime and fuel consumption significantly.

What information do you need to give a quote?

Pump motor power (kW), daily operating hours, farm location, current diesel consumption, and whether grid connection is available.
