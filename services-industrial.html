FAQ

Common questions from industrial clients

What system size does a factory typically need?

It depends on your monthly consumption and available roof or ground area. We size the system after reviewing your electricity bills and site — not from a catalogue.

Can solar keep running if the grid goes down?

A grid-tied system without batteries will shut down during an outage for safety. Adding battery storage allows the system to continue supplying critical loads independently.

How long does installation take?

Most commercial installations are completed in 1–3 weeks depending on system size, roof access, and equipment delivery lead times.
