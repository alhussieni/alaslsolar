FAQ

Common questions from homeowners

How many panels does my home need?

It depends on your monthly electricity consumption and available roof space. We calculate the right number after reviewing your bills and assessing the roof — typically 6 to 20 panels for a family home.

Will solar work during a power cut?

A standard grid-tied system shuts down during a grid outage for safety. Adding battery storage allows your home to continue running on solar and stored power when the grid is down.

How long does installation take?

Most home installations are completed in 1–2 days. We handle everything from mounting and wiring to grid connection and commissioning.

Is the roof strong enough for solar panels?

We assess the roof structure during our site visit. Modern mounting systems are lightweight and suitable for most concrete and steel roofs common in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
