What is solar energy and how does it work?

The sun every day gives us light and heat without getting tired or getting rid of itSolar energy is simply that we take part of this light and convert it into electricity, turning on the lamp, refrigerator, air conditioner, and any need for electricity in the house or work.

What is solar energy?Simply put: the sun tapers light, and we have "panels" that take this light and turn it into electricity. There is no burning of gasoline, no smoke, and no disposal. As long as the sun rises, electricity is generated.Egypt is one of the best countries in the world in this matter, because we have the sun rising almost all the year, not like other countries scattering all the time.

How does the solar panel convert light into electricity?Imagine the solar panel as a special "grid", when the light falls on it, it moves an electric current, with the same idea that the dynamism in the wheel moves and generates electricity for the lamp.You do not need to know the scientific details, what is important is that you know this simple idea:

نور شمس واقع على اللوح ← كهرباء بتتولّد جوه اللوح

This happens all day long, from the first time the sun rises until it sets.

How does electricity get you home?

There are 3 basic needs that work together:1. الألواح الشمسية – This is what takes the light and transforms it into electricity (as explained above). 2. الانفرتر – This is the same as the "interpreter", the electricity that you look at from the panels is not the same type of electricity that your devices work with, the inverter converts it to the type that the house understandsHe is able to operate the devices normally. 3. البطارية (لو موجودة) – Like a "reservoir" in which any extra electricity is stored to be used at night when the sun is setting.

Simply means: الشمس بتدّي نور → اللوح بيحوّله كهرباء → الانفرتر بيظبطها للبيت → البيت بيشتغل عادي
